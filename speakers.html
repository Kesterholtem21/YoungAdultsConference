Speakers

Meet the people teaching at LAMP.

Katie Short

Worship Leader, Youngsville Free Methodist Church

Katie serves full-time at Youngsville Free Methodist Church as the Worship Leader, where she also leads a women’s small group, the Rooted Young Adults group, and children in worship through the Avant Christian Learning Center located at the church. As a shepherd of people, she is passionate about helping others encounter Jesus, grow in their faith, and experience transformation in His presence. She finds joy in walking alongside people through every season of life and encouraging them to become all God has called them to be. Katie is a Licensed Ministerial Candidate in the Free Methodist Church and is currently pursuing ordination.

Seth Smart

Musician & songwriter, REALiFE Church (Jamestown, NY)

Seth Smart is a young adult, musician, and songwriter with a heart for pursuing Jesus and helping others grow in their faith. He serves at REALiFE Church in Jamestown, NY, where he is actively involved in the worship ministry and leads ROOTS, a young adults ministry focused on building genuine community and growing deeper in Christ. Through music, songwriting, and ministry, Seth has a passion for using the gifts God has given him to point others toward Jesus. His heart is to see this generation move beyond simply knowing about God and develop an authentic, deeply rooted relationship with Him.

Samantha Hoover

Children’s Church teacher & youth group leader

Samantha Hoover is a passionate follower of Jesus Christ with a heart for ministry, serving others, and sharing the love of God. She is actively involved in her church, where she teaches in Children’s Church, participates in small groups, and serves as one of the leaders in her youth group. She has a passion for investing in the lives of others, encouraging young people in their faith, and helping others grow in their relationship with Christ. She feels called to pursue a life of ministry and is passionate about discovering all that God has planned for her.

Avery Gates

Corry, PA

Avery Gates is from Corry, PA, and has a heart for growing deeper in her faith and sharing that journey with others. She is passionate about creating an approachable, welcoming space where people feel comfortable exploring their faith, asking questions, and growing in their relationship with God. Through sharing personal experiences, lessons learned, and honest reflections, Avery hopes to encourage others who may be walking a similar path and remind them that growth in faith is a journey worth pursuing. While she may not have all the answers, she believes there is value in being authentic, learning together, and building meaningful connections within the community. She is excited to see what God has ahead and to connect with others along the way.

Tylor Enos

Men’s Alliance & worship team

Tylor is a follower of Jesus who is passionate about helping people grow in their understanding of scriptures, defend their faith, and develop an authentic walk with Christ. He has been actively involved in the men’s ministry organization called Men’s Alliance since 2025, sharpening men to become the husbands, fathers, and leaders that God designed them to be. Through Men’s Alliance he learned how to defend his faith with the truth and share the gospel through an apologetics class. He also has been involved in music and worship ministry since he learned how to play acoustic guitar his sophomore year of college. He is actively serving on his church’s worship team.

Gavin Grieco

Leader of The Porch, Northway Community Church (Williamsport, PA)

Gavin Grieco is a 19-year-old from Williamsport, PA, who attends Northway Community Church. Although he grew up attending church, Gavin experienced a radical encounter with Jesus through Chi Alpha at the University of Pittsburgh that transformed his life. Gavin is the visionary and leader of The Porch, a Gen Z ministry through which God has reached hundreds of young people ages 15–25, seeing His Kingdom grow both deep and wide for His glory. In the midst of a significant season of ministry this past summer, Gavin was diagnosed with cancer and underwent surgery and chemotherapy. He has been in remission for several months. Gavin is currently pursuing a degree in Christian Ministries through Liberty University. His greatest passion is making disciples of Jesus who make disciples who make disciples.

Chiara Hoover

Worship team & youth ministry, Youngsville Free Methodist Church

Chiara is from Corry, Pennsylvania, and has been walking with the Lord since she was eight. She serves on the worship team at Youngsville Free Methodist Church, helps with youth ministry, and has a heart for evangelism and helping others encounter Jesus. This will be her first time speaking at a conference, and she is excited to share this opportunity alongside Avery and Sam. In January, she and Avery will also launch Faith Unfiltered, a podcast creating space for honest conversations about faith and growing in Jesus. Chiara’s heart is to live a life that points others to Him.

Brock Henderson

Youth Pastor, Salt Church (Corry, PA)

Brock Henderson serves as the youth pastor at Salt Church in Corry, Pennsylvania. He is a husband and father of four, and his love for God shapes how he approaches family life and ministry. Brock is passionate about evangelism and discipleship. He loves sharing the gospel and helping people come to know Jesus Christ personally. He also finds joy in walking alongside others as they grow in their faith and learn what it means to follow Jesus in everyday life.
