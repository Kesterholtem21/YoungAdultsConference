Speakers

Meet the people leading worship and teaching at LAMP.

Katie Short

Worship Leader, Youngsville Free Methodist Church

Katie serves full-time at Youngsville Free Methodist Church as the Worship Leader, where she also leads a women’s small group, the Rooted Young Adults group, and children in worship through the Avant Christian Learning Center located at the church. As a shepherd of people, she is passionate about helping others encounter Jesus, grow in their faith, and experience transformation in His presence. She finds joy in walking alongside people through every season of life and encouraging them to become all God has called them to be. Katie is a Licensed Ministerial Candidate in the Free Methodist Church and is currently pursuing ordination.

Seth Smart

Musician & songwriter, REALiFE Church (Jamestown, NY)

Seth Smart is a young adult, musician, and songwriter with a heart for pursuing Jesus and helping others grow in their faith. He serves at REALiFE Church in Jamestown, NY, where he is actively involved in the worship ministry and leads ROOTS, a young adults ministry focused on building genuine community and growing deeper in Christ. Through music, songwriting, and ministry, Seth has a passion for using the gifts God has given him to point others toward Jesus. His heart is to see this generation move beyond simply knowing about God and develop an authentic, deeply rooted relationship with Him.

Samantha Hoover

Children’s Church teacher & youth group leader

Samantha Hoover is a passionate follower of Jesus Christ with a heart for ministry, serving others, and sharing the love of God. She is actively involved in her church, where she teaches in Children’s Church, participates in small groups, and serves as one of the leaders in her youth group. She has a passion for investing in the lives of others, encouraging young people in their faith, and helping others grow in their relationship with Christ. She feels called to pursue a life of ministry and is passionate about discovering all that God has planned for her.
