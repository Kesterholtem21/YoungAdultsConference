Privacy Policy

What this website and the registration form do with your information.

Last updated: TBD

Who we are

LAMP (Long After Midnight Passes) is a young adults conference hosted by TBD. In this policy, “we” means the LAMP organizing team. If you have a question about your information, see Contact us below.

The short version

  • This website has no accounts, no ads and no visitor tracking or analytics.
  • This website does not set any cookies of its own.
  • We only collect personal information when you register, and we use it only to run the conference.
  • We never sell your information.

When you just visit the site

Like every website, a few services see basic technical details (such as your IP address and browser type) when they deliver pages to you:

  • Hosting (GitHub Pages). The site is hosted by GitHub. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. We don’t have access to these logs. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts). Our fonts load from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address, the page address and your browser details so it can send the font files. Google Fonts does not set cookies.

When you register

The registration form asks for:

  • Your name and contact information
  • Your room choice and any roommate request
  • Accessibility needs and dietary restrictions
  • Which labs you want to attend
  • Payment for your room

We use this information only to plan and run LAMP: arranging rooms and meals, accommodating accessibility needs, organizing labs and contacting you about the event. Only the organizing team sees it. We treat accessibility and dietary details with extra care and share them only with the people who need them to serve you (for example, the kitchen or venue staff).

The form is provided by TBD and payments are handled by TBD. Your answers are stored with that service under its own privacy policy. We do not see or store your full card details.

We keep registration information until TBD after the conference, then delete it, unless you ask us to keep you on a mailing list.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies. When the registration form opens, it is shown from TBD’s website, and that service may set its own cookies while you fill it in. Those cookies are covered by its privacy policy. If we ever add something that uses cookies (such as visitor analytics or embedded videos), we will update this policy first.

Ages

LAMP is a conference for young adults. This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Age requirements for attending: TBD

Links to other sites

This site links to other websites (such as the registration form and church pages). Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.
