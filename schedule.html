Labs

Pick one option in each lab block. Lab 1 is Saturday at 9:00 AM and Lab 2 is at 10:15 AM.

Counterpressure

Lab 1 · How to defend our truth in a culture that is increasingly pressing against what we know is truth.

Pressed, Not Crushed

Lab 1 · His testimony of battling cancer, and how to let the pressing from suffering produce the oil for our lamps instead of losing hope and faith.

Wise Company

Lab 2 · Learn how to surround yourself with people who help keep your fire burning.

Keeping the Oil

Lab 2 · Build the disciplines that keep your heart burning, and break the excuses, distractions, and busyness that keep us from God.
